What to disrupt

Protected

    No tool can make an image impossible to train on. Mistcoat raises the cost and spoils the result; it is not a guarantee. Always keep your unprotected originals, and upload only the protected copies.

    What Mistcoat does

    It makes small, mostly invisible changes that degrade an image's value as AI training data. It disrupts the fine texture statistics models learn from, scrambles the local stroke patterns that make up your style, perturbs colour where the eye cannot follow, and writes the change into the parts of the file that survive being re-compressed.

    It also embeds the IPTC do not mine metadata, a machine-readable statement that the work is not licensed for AI training.

    What it does not do

    It cannot make an image impossible to train on. A determined party can reduce or defeat any protection of this kind; published research has broken every tool in this category to some degree. Treat it as raising the cost, not as a lock.
